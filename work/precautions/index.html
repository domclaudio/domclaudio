A marketing campaign for a drug that doesn't exist, for a film that hasn't been shot yet.

Forum thread · Patient leaflet · Pharmacy listing · Corporate site · Case file
Problem
A no-budget horror short has no audience before it's shot, and a crowdfunding campaign needs one before launch. Trailers need footage. Ads need money.
Insight
Horror fans don't want to be told about a film; they want to find evidence of it. And pharma marketing is a format everyone already knows how to read: the patient forum, the leaflet, the fine print.
Idea
Market the drug, not the movie. Give Springwood Pharmaceuticals and Leverazopam a real-looking presence online, so the world of the film exists before a frame of it does.

The film is about a family that can't say the thing out loud, so the marketing doesn't either.

How It Spreads

Nothing explains itself

The clues stay in the data. Nothing in the thread, the leaflet or the case file explains itself. The details reward anyone who checks them, and finding one is something to post about. That's how the campaign spreads.

Important safety information

Leverazopam and Springwood Pharmaceuticals are fictional. The campaign site discloses the fiction and credits the film.
